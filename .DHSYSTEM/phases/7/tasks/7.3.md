## Best Practices

- Say “no API key” or “no provider API fee” instead of promising unlimited free use.
- State that local models require download, disk, RAM/CPU/GPU and may carry their own license terms.
- State that no-key online adapters require Internet and can be rate-limited, changed or unavailable.
- State that provider APIs may require an account/key and can be billable even when a temporary trial/free tier exists.
- Keep volatile prices and quota numbers out of product strings; link to official current pricing in user-facing handoff when needed.
- Preserve the exact provider registries and Chinese media-language support.
